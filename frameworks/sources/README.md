# Captured sources

Primary sources kept in the repository so a reader can check a transcription rather than
trust it.

## mit-harm-severity-2026-10-10.png and .pdf

MIT AI Risk Initiative, AI Incident Tracker, Harm Taxonomy.
URL: https://airisk.mit.edu/ai-incident-tracker/harm-taxonomy
Captured 10 October 2026. Data licensed CC BY 4.0.

**The PNG is the source of record. The PDF is the cross-check, not the other way round.**

The PDF was printed from the page and the table runs off the right edge of the paper.
Page 2 shows roughly seven of the ten harm columns and no header row at all, so the
category names are not in it. The PNG carries the full five levels by ten harm types
with its headers, and the two Negligible cells expected to be hidden behind a tooltip,
Democratic Norms and Privacy, are both legible in it.

The seven columns the PDF does show were compared against the PNG and agree.

MIT's own footnote on the table, transcribed because it changes what the categories mean:

> Categories are based on the CSET Taxonomy of AI Harm. I have removed the 'Additional
> Definitions' and 'Other' categories from the CSET taxonomy and changed the
> 'Detrimental Content' category to 'Toxic or Malicious Content'.

So this is MIT's modification of CSET rather than CSET itself, and the register says so.
